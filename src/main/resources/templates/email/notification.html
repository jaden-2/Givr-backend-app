I'm back, and I'd like to think you missed these. Even if it's a little one-sided.

This week I have something worth celebrating, and honestly, who better to share it with than you?

The Greener Basket for Nutrition and Agriculture Foundation (GBNAF) just returned to Givr.

GBNAF was one of the very first organisations to post a project on the platform— about three days after we went live.

I won't lie to you. I had at least five panic attacks the day the project went up, and about ten on the day of the outreach. We were fresh out of the box, and they were technically our first real test.

Fortunately, the volunteers showed up and showed out. The reviews were glowing. And now GBNAF is back.

This week's outreach 💙

This time around we visited an IDP camp. It was beautiful and stressful in equal measure—which honestly feels like the right combination for work that matters.

Our photographer volunteer came with his own camera and captured some incredible moments. A few people cancelled, but the ones who showed up were exactly the kind of people Givr exists for.
